09 · The person

About me.

Then

I spent way too long in academia studying informal logic (how we argue), rhetorical persuasion (what arguments win), and meaning-making (why anything matters). My Ph.D. in Philosophy from McMaster focused on human dignity. Most people don't know that many scholars consider it a “useless concept.” Defending something dismissed as useless by people much smarter than me turned out to be good training for marketing.

Defending something dismissed as useless by people much smarter than me turned out to be good training for marketing.

My graduate research centered on environmental ethics. Questions like: what does saying “I was treated like an animal” tell us about our conceptions of animality? (It's not good.) Why do otherwise normal, upstanding citizens have out-of-morals experiences,1 where we dismiss the suffering of sentient mammals?

That work led to ethics consulting at McMaster's Institute on Ethics & Policy for Innovation, the institute that delivers ethics consultation for the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. I wrote on the ethics of CRISPR-related gene drive technologies, translating controversial but life-saving biotech into policy recommendations.

Stuff happened.

Now

Now I write for Fortune 500 executives. 150 deliverables for the former CEO of Insight, Joyce Mullen, across two years. 30+ for Jack Azagury, current CEO of Insight, in his first six weeks after 29 years at Accenture. My job is to make complex ideas land for highly technical audiences: a keynote for 1,500 salespeople, a two-minute video that closes a $19M deal, the list goes on and the deadlines never get longer.

If you've read this far, congrats. You're a real one. But now it's time to engage with the page. I mean, for god's sake, tap or click something.
